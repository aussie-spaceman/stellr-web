## Blast radius

- **Migrations:** five (below), applied to production first.
- **`vercel.json`:** unchanged; no cron starts or stops.
- **New environment variables:** `ESIGN_*`, `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `MEMBERSHIP_AGREEMENT_ENFORCE`. **None are needed for this promotion.** Without them the signing engine stays inert, and the membership gate stays report-only. They are needed before the 9 Oct canary (handover step 2).
- **Webhooks:** `app/api/webhooks/docusign/route.ts` changed (#273, provider seam). `app/api/webhooks/resend/route.ts` is new; it does nothing until Resend is pointed at it (handover step 3).
- **Behavior changes for live users:**
  - Fresh self-sign-ups by Minors are refused at onboarding (403 `minor_self_signup_closed`); admin-invited, event-registered or consented Minors are unaffected.
  - Credential pages can't be made public for under-13s or unknown DOB.
  - Ads consent is forced off on student pages and in student-marked browsers; HubSpot tracking is off for signed-in students.
  - Private-link pages (sign, pay, join) load no tags.
  - Membership Agreement emails are dispatched at onboarding (non-fatal; native engine inert, so expect logged failures until it is configured).
- **Outbound mail:** e-sign outbox and reminders are inert in `docusign_only` mode.
