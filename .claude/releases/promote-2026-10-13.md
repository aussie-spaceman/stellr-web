## What ships
- **#402: HubSpot event options follow Sanity.**
  - Location, Year, Theme and Demographic values are derived from the Sanity event, with a new optional Studio field "HubSpot location" as an override.
  - `lib/hubspot-event-options.ts` reconciles the option lists on contacts and deals. Retired options are hidden, never deleted, and the Event Notify form's option copies are refreshed.
  - The sync runs from the Sanity event-sync webhook (real production app only) and from a new daily cron.
  - `captureLead` now merges the event multi-selects into a contact's history instead of overwriting them.
