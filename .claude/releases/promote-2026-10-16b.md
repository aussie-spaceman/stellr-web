## What ships
The three phases of the Image Optimization plan. On 16 Oct, 75% of the Hobby team's 5,000 monthly image transformations was already used.

- **#436 Phase 1, `next.config.mjs` images config** (`0aaf19a4`):
  - WebP only (AVIF dropped). Fewer widths: device 640/828/1080/1200/1920, image 64/128/256/384. Quality 75 only. 31-day `minimumCacheTTL`.
  - `remotePatterns` allows only our Sanity project/dataset path and the media host, both read from env. The Unsplash, Clerk and wildcard Blob entries are gone.
  - `localPatterns` lists the folders next/image loads (`/api/img`, `/images`, `/team`, `/media`, `/videos`). `dangerouslyAllowSVG` is removed.
  - The Clerk avatar on `/account` is `unoptimized`.
  - `images.unoptimized` is on for the `stellr-web-dev` project only, matched by `VERCEL_PROJECT_ID`.
- **#437 Phase 2, Sanity images skip the optimizer** (`590599c7`):
  - New `CoverImage` (a plain `<img>`) and `lib/sanity-image.ts`. Each Sanity image is resized, cropped and turned into WebP q75 by Sanity's CDN, then watermarked once by `/api/img`.
  - `/api/img` now serves only the preset widths and transforms the site emits. Anything else gets a 400. The project/dataset pin, size cap and fail-closed watermark are unchanged.
  - Call sites: event and campaign cards, `/events`, `/events/[slug]` hero, `/news`, `/news/[slug]`, home page event cards.
- **#439 Phase 3, pre-sized slides and posters** (`fd7bccaf`):
  - The student-work slides and all nine video posters render from WebP copies cut once at upload (640/1080/1600, capped at the source width), as plain `<img srcset>`.
  - `scripts/upload-media.ts` makes those copies. The 33 copies were uploaded to `stellr-media` on 16 Oct and approved in that session.
- **#440 Skills:** ship/promote/close-out wait on CI in the desktop app with one "CI finished" button. ship and promote check for stranded docs PRs at the start.
- **#438:** close-out docs for the worktree-sweep session (handover + tracker).
