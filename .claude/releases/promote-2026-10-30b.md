## What ships

`origin/main..origin/dev` has one commit:

- #484 `2b6970f3`: **Check-in desk: group check-in, scanning, offline desk and arrival flags.** This follows the check-in app review (30 Oct 2026), which recommended no native iOS app.
  - The desk console gets a Groups view. A school group is checked in as a unit: untick whoever isn't here, and Undo is available straight after.
  - Signed check-in codes for each participant and each group. They appear in confirmation emails, on the teacher's "Your group" page and, optionally, on badges.
  - A camera scanner in the desk, using the WASM barcode reader.
  - Offline desk:
    - taps are queued on the device with their own time;
    - the last list is kept on the device;
    - in desk mode a service worker keeps the page for a reload with no connection.
  - Staff-only flags (agreement not signed, unpaid, medical, dietary). They never block check-in.
  - Badges can print in school order.
