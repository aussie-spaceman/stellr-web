## What ships
- **Group invoices (#554):** any number of roster changes. A roster change now waits for 2 quiet minutes before the unpaid invoice is updated, and one email goes out per burst. When Stripe refuses a further revision, the invoice is replaced. Admin gets "Send corrected invoice". A new daily cron is the backstop.
- **Individual registration (#555):** an email or school address on file is asked to sign in, and nothing is written. A signed-in date of birth that differs from the account's is refused, with a way forward.
- **Group registration (#558):** the same rule for the organiser. People an organiser enters are not asked.
- **Active CPU phase 2 (#556):** prefetch off on public pages, cheaper watermark on `/api/img`, photo removal by cache tag, 6 hour caches on public pages, Sanity reads and price caches. Check-in desk polls every 10 s.
- **Proxy matcher (#553):** gated prefixes always run the proxy, whatever the path's extension. `/_vercel/` is skipped.
- **Dependencies (#550):** minimatch and glob overrides, OSV config and `security.yml`.
- **Docs only:** #552, #557, and the previous promotion record #551.
