## What ships
- **Change how a group pays (#573):** admins, event managers assigned to the event, teachers and Teacher POCs can move an unpaid group between the three existing ways to pay: invoice, card, or each member individually. No new payment type. Only the unpaid balance moves; places paid individually stay paid, and a group whose own payment is taken can't be changed. The old way to pay is closed first (open Checkout sessions expired, open invoices voided). Roster: "Payment method…". Account → Teams: "How this group pays".
- **Pay links work whatever the event's registration status (#573):** the closed-registration check is gone from the pay and pay-seat routes and pages, for individual registrations as well as groups. The owner's decision: closing registration stops new sign-ups, not payment for a place already held.
- **Group registration refuses a priced event whose price can't be read (#570):** answers 503 before anything is written, instead of recording a group that owes $0. Campaigns are exempt.
- **Test only (#572):** field crypto test checks the ciphertext form.
- **Docs only:** #569, #571, and the previous promotion record #568.
