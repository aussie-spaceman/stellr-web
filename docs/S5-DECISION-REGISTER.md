# S5 decision register

One list of the owner decisions that S5 wave 7 depends on, grouped by the gate each one blocks. Every entry has an id, the question in full, the safe default we recommend, the answer, and the date it was answered (or "open"). Owner questions cite these ids, and only ids that exist here.

This is a new file so that it never collides with `docs/TENANCY-S5.md`, which stays the plan of record. Decisions already written up elsewhere are linked, not copied (see [Settled elsewhere](#settled-elsewhere)). Open questions go to the owner about 5 at a time, each with its default. Tracker row `s5-programme.41` points here. Batch 2 was answered on 12 Nov 2026, with the merges of A10a (#655) and A10b (#660). Batch 3 (`W7-14` and `W7-17` to `W7-32`) and the remaining questions from wave 7 PR bodies were answered on 13 Nov 2026. The row stays open until the rest of the decisions are added.

Started 12 Nov 2026; batch 2, `W7-16` and the PR-review answers recorded on 12 Nov 2026 (docs train D2); batch 3, the dev canary zone and the rest of the PR-review answers recorded on 13 Nov 2026 (docs train D3). People are named by role only: "the owner" is the programme's owner.

## How to read an entry

- **Id:** `W7-<n>`. `W7-1` to `W7-8` keep the numbers of the wave 7 plan's "Owner actions and decisions first" list, so either numbering can be cited.
- **Recommended default:** the answer we recommend. For an open entry that gates code (batch 2), it is also what each slice builds until the owner answers, and those defaults fail closed. For an entry that gates a hosted step (`W7-1` to `W7-8`, and `W7-14`), nothing happens until the owner answers: a "Yes" here is a recommendation, never a yes to act.
- **Answer:** "Approved as recommended", "Declined", or "Open", with the date. Where the owner answered in a few words, those words are quoted and the entry says how they are read.
