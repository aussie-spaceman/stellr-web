The Fall Space Design Campaign is open to high school classes and clubs across the US, and entry is free.

**What it is.** Your class becomes an aerospace engineering company responding to a client’s Request for Proposal. Students split into departments, research and weigh design options, and write one proposal as a team. It runs in class or as a club, at your pace.

**What you get, free:**
- The Campaign Request for Proposal and Mission Handbook (abridged editions)
- A teacher Campaign guide with 4-week and 10-week schedules
- A student Campaign guide and assessment tools
- Written feedback from our judging panel on every proposal submitted before the deadline

**What you need.** A classroom and internet access. No lab, no kit, and no engineering background — the guides are written for teachers who are not engineers.

**Key dates.** The Campaign runs from 15 August to 15 December 2026. Proposals are due **11 December 2026**, and you can replace your upload as many times as you like until then.

**Who can enter.** High school students, grades 9–12. A teacher, mentor or student manager registers the group, and a school can enter as many groups as it likes.

New to running a design challenge? Our guide, [How to run an engineering design challenge in your classroom](/guides/run-an-engineering-design-challenge), walks through the eight stages, your first session and how to assess.
