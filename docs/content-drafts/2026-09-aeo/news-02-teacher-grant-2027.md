Taking a team to a competition, or running one at your school, takes real time: recruiting students, coordinating logistics, following through afterward. The Teacher Grant Program pays you for it.

**What you receive in 2027**

| What you do | What you receive |
|---|---|
| Onboarding — orientation, agreement, a baseline survey and a one-page plan | $50 |
| Bring a team (at least 5 students plus you) to a live Challenge | $200 |
| Run a Campaign at your school with at least 8 students registered | $200 |
| Close out — your survey and reflection, and two-thirds of your students’ | $50 |
| **Maximum per year** | **$500** |

Payment is a single check, posted 31 May.

**Beyond the money:** 10–20 documented PD contact hours with a statement of the standards covered, free Catalyst membership for the year (a $149 value), a certificate and LinkedIn badge, and a letter of recommendation on request.

**Who can apply.** US high school teachers. Expect 10–20 hours across the year. There are **15 places** for 2027, and we reply to every application.
