The Privacy Policy and Terms (Last Updated 09-Oct-2026) now say who can open a private credential page, including anyone holding the link in the issued email. The guardian line in that email no longer implies an under-13 or declined page will go public. G1 failed: the code treated anyone 18 or over as an adult, while the policy's "Minor" uses the state age of majority and anyone still in school. David chose the code fix (9 Oct), so credentials now use `isMinorPerPolicy` (`lib/minor-policy.ts`) with the school's state, grade and age bracket. This applies at issue, in the live consent check, and for who the issued and revoked emails go to.
