A group's invoice now follows its roster: removing a participant revises an unpaid invoice (or refunds a paid place under the 45-day guarantee, with a credit note), adding beyond the group's places revises it or raises an additional invoice, admins can apply a discount code from the roster, the Billing tab shows invoice history and refunds, and members can withdraw an individual registration themselves. First used in production on the Minnesota group (registration `0e416e3b-8a3d-4348-b1fb-88e4f3873ff9`).

## Closes

- `12.2` (refund basis is the event Price, not the charge): refunds now use what the place cost — a paid group invoice's total ÷ its places, or the Checkout fee line's total ÷ its quantity (`lib/refunds/payment-basis.ts`), with Stripe's remaining-refundable cap on top. Verified in the Stripe test-mode drill ($82.45 refunded on a 15%-discounted $97 place) and by `lib/refunds/execute.test.ts`. Residual: a group card payment with no stored `checkout_session_id` still falls back to list price (capped by the charge's remaining balance). Account-credit refunds no longer exist under the guarantee.
